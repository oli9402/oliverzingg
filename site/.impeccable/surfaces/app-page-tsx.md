# Home surface (/)

Scope: home page of the portfolio + wiki site. Visitor mode: Experience (portfolio), with Read pages (wiki, project write-ups) inheriting the same world.
Audience: data-science hiring managers and recruiters in Switzerland; job: judge range and fit in seconds, then reach CV, projects or contact.
Proof: real theses, projects, wiki notes, CV facts (see PRODUCT.md). No invented metrics.
Chosen by the user after three re-rolls: the category standard, elevated. Mix of mock C (colour field) intro + mock A (dual-track timeline) + mock C tabbed index. Mocks: .impeccable/mocks/home/.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A classic portfolio made unmistakable by commitment: a full-bleed ultramarine field and monumental wide type say "confident, direct". Refuses the soft centred-hero-plus-equal-cards template.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, ink #101322, ultramarine #2340D9 owning the intro field and every accent, signal yellow #FFD84D reserved for "now" and the primary action, dark ink strip for the status bar. Archivo throughout; display set at wdth 125, weight 800, uppercase for the name, 112% for section heads. Square-ish 6px radii, hairline #E4E5EA rules, no shadows except the active tab.

STORY: Visitor learns who (psychologist + data scientist, Zürich), sees the path (education above / work below one 2018–2026 axis with a today marker), browses work, notes, roles in one tabbed index, then emails or downloads the CV.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Ultramarine field: nav row; left, name in two uppercase lines at ~9vw; one bold sentence + supporting line; yellow "See my work" + outlined "Download CV". Right, tall portrait bleeding to the field's bottom edge. Below the fold line, the dark status strip (Now: Zurich Insurance, HSLU, UZH, Available 2027).

FORM: Canon (category standard executed at full craft), position: standing exit taken after three rolls. seed:9a7ad57d874087c2

Signature interaction: the dual-track timeline (hover lifts a bar and links to its entry), and the tabbed index switching Projects / Knowledge / Work / Education without reload. Motion grammar: short exponential ease-out lifts and image scale on hover; no scroll-triggered entrances.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
