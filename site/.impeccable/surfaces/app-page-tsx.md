# Home surface (/)

Scope: home page of the portfolio + wiki site. Visitor mode: Experience (portfolio). Reading pages inherit the same world in a calmer form.
Audience: data-science hiring managers and recruiters in Switzerland; job: judge range and fit in about 90 seconds, then reach the CV, projects or contact.
Proof: real theses, projects, wiki notes, CV facts (see PRODUCT.md). No invented metrics.
History: the clean ultramarine canon was built first (seed:9a7ad57d874087c2). On 2026-11-07 the user pinned a new direction: a neon 16-bit Blade-Runner-style night city as the default, with the clean design kept behind a toggle. A user-pinned direction beats the roll.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Oliver's file, read at night in a living 16-bit Zürich: a pixel city that follows the real time and weather, with every fact from his CV and projects built into the world. Refuses the dark-hacker cliché by putting plain words, a sharp photo and a 30-second dossier in front of the atmosphere.

OWN-WORLD: Void #07040f ground, solid panels with 4px violet frames, amber primary actions and "now", cyan links and focus, pink markers. Press Start 2P for short labels, Pixelify Sans for long titles, VT323 for UI copy, JetBrains Mono for reading. Hard pixel bevels, CRT scanlines, rooftop parallax city, rain-streaked window behind reading pages.

STORY: The visitor boots in (once per session) and picks quick dossier, full record or explore. They see a big pixel name and a sharp portrait with a CLEARED FOR HIRE stamp, read the case files on a CRT monitor wall, follow the mission-log timeline to a NEXT? slot, skim the databank, then email, open the CV, LinkedIn or GitHub from the neon billboard.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Opaque HUD with plain nav plus DOSSIER and CLEAN MODE; left: OLIVER / ZINGG in Press Start 2P with chromatic shadow, typewriter line, a one-paragraph lede, ACCESS FILES (amber) and VIEW RECORD (ghost); right: an ID card with the full-resolution portrait (one yellow 16-bit scan on load, replay on hover), with SUBJECT, CLASS, SECTOR and STATUS rows. City and rain behind.

FORM: User-pinned direction (neon 16-bit city), replacing the canon round's clean world as the default; clean kept as the secondary theme. seed:9a7ad57d874087c2

Signature interaction: the live Zürich city (time of day, weather), the rain-sweep page transition with the card-to-article morph, and the terminal on the backtick key.
