<section id="attitude-questions" class="level1">

<p>Definition:</p>
<ul>
<li>In theory, not verifiable by external observation or records.</li>
<li>Has evaluative component</li>
<li>How strong agree or disagree</li>
<li>In contrast to beliefs (cognitive component): How healthy is a pizza?</li>
<li>In contrast to behavioral intentions: In the next 6 months, how likely is it that you will buy a car</li>
</ul>
<section id="traditional-view-on-attitude" class="level2">
<h2 class="anchored" data-anchor-id="traditional-view-on-attitude">Traditional View on Attitude</h2>
<p>A psychological tendency that is expressed by evaluating a particular entity with some degree of favor or disfavor</p>
<p>Attitudes are Pre-existing: - Automatic activation of attitudes (retrievable: “file-drawer”) - stable and predictive of behaviors, resistant to persuasion</p>
<section id="problems-of-traditional-view" class="level3">
<h3 class="anchored" data-anchor-id="problems-of-traditional-view">Problems of traditional view</h3>
<p>In survey big differences are found depending on how question is asked.</p>
</section>
</section>
<section id="alternative-view" class="level2">
<h2 class="anchored" data-anchor-id="alternative-view">Alternative View</h2>
<p>“Making it up as you go along”</p>
<ul>
<li>individuals do not typically possess true attitudes rather they construct opinion statements on the fly based on whatever considerations are momentarily salient</li>
</ul>
<p>Schwarz 2007: evaluative judgments formed on the spot rather than as trait-like dispositions</p>
</section>
<section id="rasinksis-way-resovling-these-divergent-views" class="level2">
<h2 class="anchored" data-anchor-id="rasinksis-way-resovling-these-divergent-views">Rasinksi’s way: Resovling these Divergent views</h2>
<p>Attitudes vs.&nbsp;attitude expressions</p>
<ul>
<li>Any single attitude measure will be an imperfect reflection of the underlying attitude</li>
<li>Attitudes are unobservable, global evaluations of objects</li>
</ul>
<p>Some attitude expressions are construced on the spot, some are retrieved almost entirely intact from memory, others are a combination.</p>
</section>
<section id="schwarz-resolving" class="level2">
<h2 class="anchored" data-anchor-id="schwarz-resolving">Schwarz Resolving</h2>
<p>No attitudes, but contextualized construction of evaluations/judgments on the spot drawing on what is available to Respondent (R)</p>
<ul>
<li>Stable attitudes = similar evalutions arising from R drawing on similar inputs or the some chronically accessible information.</li>
<li>Strong attitudes = R drawing on highly accessible information</li>
<li>Context effects = R drawing on contextual information when constructing evaluations</li>
<li>Nonattitude = Same number of conflicting information accessible to R at the same time</li>
</ul>
</section>

<section id="context-effects-comprehension" class="level3">
<h3 class="anchored" data-anchor-id="context-effects-comprehension">Context Effects: Comprehension</h3>
<ul>
<li>Assimilation effects: Rs include prior question in interpreting/understanding the current item</li>
<li>Contrast effects: Rs exclude prior questions in interpreting and understanding the current item</li>
</ul>
