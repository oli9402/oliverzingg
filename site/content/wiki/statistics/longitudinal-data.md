<section id="latent-class-growth-analysis" class="level2">
<h2 class="anchored" data-anchor-id="latent-class-growth-analysis">Latent Class Growth Analysis</h2>
<ul>
<li><p>Groups individuals into distinct trajectory classes over time.</p></li>
<li><p>Individuals in the same class follow a similar longitudinal pattern.</p></li>
<li><p>No within-class variance (trajectories are homogeneous within each class).</p></li>
<li><p>Clear, discrete groups of longitudinal patterns.</p></li>
<li><p>https://www.statisticalinnovations.com/wp-content/uploads/Magidson_2020_LatentClassAnalysis.pdf ## Growth Mixture Modeling (GMM)</p></li>
</ul>
</section>
<section id="hidden-markov-models-hmms" class="level2">
<h2 class="anchored" data-anchor-id="hidden-markov-models-hmms">Hidden Markov Models (HMMs)</h2>
<ul>
<li>Individuals transition between latent states over time</li>
<li>Focus is on state switching, not stable trajectory groups</li>
<li>Good for behavioral or clinical state changes</li>
</ul>
