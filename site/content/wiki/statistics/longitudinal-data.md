## Latent Class Growth Analysis

-   Groups individuals into distinct trajectory classes over time.

-   Individuals in the same class follow a similar longitudinal pattern.

-   No within-class variance (trajectories are homogeneous within each class).

-   Clear, discrete groups of longitudinal patterns.

-   https://www.statisticalinnovations.com/wp-content/uploads/Magidson\_2020\_LatentClassAnalysis.pdf ## Growth Mixture Modeling (GMM)


## Hidden Markov Models (HMMs)

-   Individuals transition between latent states over time
-   Focus is on state switching, not stable trajectory groups
-   Good for behavioral or clinical state changes
